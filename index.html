About

How I work

  • Defined risk on every trade

    Each trade is sent with a stop loss and a take profit, and a drawdown limit pauses trading if the account falls too far.

  • Control without the settings window

    A compact panel on the chart shows status and open trades, and lets you change risk, direction and limits as you go.

  • Plain, honest documentation

    Each Expert Advisor comes with a user manual and recommended settings. Backtests are labelled as backtests, never presented as live results.
