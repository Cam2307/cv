02 · On the chart

Control that lives where you trade.

A small panel in the corner of the chart shows each Expert Advisor’s status and open trades, and opens into tabs when you need more.

  1. 01

    Defined exits

    Every trade is sent with a stop loss and a take profit from the moment it opens.

  2. 02

    Risk you choose

    Pick Conservative, Balanced, Aggressive or Custom, and change it on the chart without opening the settings window.

  3. 03

    A drawdown limit

    If the account falls by the limit you set, the Expert Advisor closes its own trades and pauses.

  4. 04

    Direction, spread and sessions

    Trade both ways or one way only, skip wide spreads, and limit trading to the London and New York sessions if you prefer.

  5. 05

    Close all, asked twice

    Close all closes only that Expert Advisor’s own trades, and asks for a second click to confirm.

  6. 06

    Documented properly

    Each comes with a user manual and recommended settings on its MQL5 product page.
